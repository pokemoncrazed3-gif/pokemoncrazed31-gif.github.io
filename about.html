Our Story

When we opened, we were just a small, quiet restaurant that many people overlooked. Slowly as more and more people came and checked us out, we grew. People started talking about us and our fine food, getting more people interested. Today, we are a busy, bustling restaurant known for our great food!

Chef Emilia Roux

Biography

Chef Roux's culinary adventure began at the prestigious Institute Paul Bocuse in Lyon, where she honed her skills under the tutelage of renowned chefs. Her insatiable curiosity and love for global cuisines led her to travel across Europe and Asia, gathering a palette of flavors, techniques, and inspirations.

Philosophy
